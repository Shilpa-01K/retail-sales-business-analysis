# Retail Sales and Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, product categories, regional performance, and sales channels.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Excel data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive dashboard.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Understand sales trends across 2024 and 2025.
- Evaluate sales performance by sales channel.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Formulas and Functions
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Data Cleaning and Data Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## Data Cleaning and Preparation
The dataset was prepared using Excel. Key activities included:
- Identifying and removing duplicate records.
- Checking and handling missing values.
- Formatting date and numerical columns.
- Creating calculated columns for business analysis.

## Key Calculated Columns
- **Gross Sales:** Quantity × Unit Price
- **Discount Amount:** Gross Sales × Discount Rate
- **Net Sales:** Gross Sales − Discount Amount
- **Total Cost:** Quantity × Unit Cost
- **Profit:** Net Sales − Total Cost
- **Profit Margin:** Profit ÷ Net Sales

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard tracks:
- Total Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Profit Margin

## Dashboard Features
- Net Sales by Product Category
- Monthly Net Sales Trend (2024 vs. 2025)
- Total Profit by Region
- Net Sales by Sales Channel
- Interactive Category and Region slicers

## Project Results
The completed analysis reports:

- **Total Profit:** ₹11,47,457.25

- **Average Profit Margin:** 38.27%
